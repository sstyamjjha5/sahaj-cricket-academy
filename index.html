FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about joining SCCA.

Is Sahaj Chadha a certified cricket coach?

Yes. Sahaj Chadha is an ECB (England & Wales Cricket Board) Level 2 Certified Cricket Coach, with a decade of playing and coaching experience across the UK, South Africa, and internationally for the Cambodia National Team.

Can I train with Sahaj Chadha Cricket Academy from outside India?

Yes. The academy offers online coaching worldwide, including video analysis, fitness plans, and live tactical Zoom sessions designed for players who don't have access to in-person nets.

What makes Sahaj Chadha's coaching different from other academies?

Sahaj combines international playing experience (Cambodia National Team, UK and South Africa club cricket) with a formal ECB Level 2 coaching certification bringing a data-informed, tactical approach backed by performance stats from sources like ESPNcricinfo, Cricbuzz, and the ICC.

Is there a registration fee to join the academy?

No. There is no one-time registration fee. You only pay per session or program based on the tier you choose — Group, 1-on-1 Personal, High Performance, or Online Training.

Where is Sahaj Chadha Cricket Academy located?

Sahaj Chadha Cricket Academy operates globally offering online coaching to students anywhere in the world, alongside in-person high-performance sessions. Coach Sahaj has trained players across the UK, South Africa, and India. To find the nearest in-person session or start online, reach out via WhatsApp or phone.

What age groups do you coach at the academy?

We cater to all age groups. Our Development program starts at U-12 (Under 12 years), while our Competitive and High-Performance programs are designed for active league players of all ages up to senior levels.

Do you offer personal 1-on-1 coaching?

Yes. Our 'Personal Training' and 'High Performance' tiers are exclusively dedicated to one-on-one sessions. This includes video analysis, personalized diet plans, and advanced biomechanics correction directly with Coach Sahaj.
